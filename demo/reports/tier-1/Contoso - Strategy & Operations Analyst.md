## B) Gaps and opportunities
**Cost allocation.** They want someone who can "build and maintain cost-allocation models for shared services". You have built pricing and forecasting models, not allocation keys, but the logic is the same: drivers, assumptions and a reconciliation back to the ledger. Rebuilding one coursework model as an allocation model over a weekend, with a one-page note on the assumptions, gives you something concrete to walk through in the case round.

Nothing else here needs closing before you apply.

Apply this week and lead with the markdown-pricing model from your internship: it shows you can turn a model into a decision a business team acts on, which is exactly what the quarterly pack is for. It does pull you a little away from pricing; if pricing stays your first choice, treat this as the broader door rather than the narrower one.
